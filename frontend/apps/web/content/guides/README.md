# TechOffice user guides

TechOffice is one workspace for a small business: the people, the routine work that has to
happen every day, the conversation about it, the schedule, and the written procedures —
all in the same place, connected to each other.

The point is not that it has chat, or a checklist, or a calendar. Plenty of tools have
those, and most small businesses end up running four of them at once. The point is that a
message about the fridge temperature, the checklist item that recorded it, the shift it
happened on, and the procedure that says why 4°C matters are the *same* piece of work in
TechOffice, not four disconnected copies of it in four apps.

## The example used throughout

Every guide uses the same fictional business so the screenshots line up:

**Bright Bean Coffee** — two stores and a small head office.

| Person | Role | How they sign in |
|---|---|---|
| Dana Whitfield | Owner, runs head office | Email and password |
| Mai Tran | Store manager, Riverside | Account ID `mai.tran` + 6-digit PIN |
| Leo Alvarez | Barista, Riverside | Account ID `leo.alvarez` + 6-digit PIN |
| Priya Nair | Store manager, Old Town | Account ID `priya.nair` + 6-digit PIN |
| Sam Okafor | Barista, Old Town | Account ID `sam.okafor` + 6-digit PIN |

That split — a couple of people with email addresses, and everyone else without one — is
the normal shape of a small business, and TechOffice is built for it. Your baristas,
drivers, cleaners and shop-floor staff do not need a company email account to use
TechOffice.

## Two words you will see everywhere

**Project** — a container for work. Bright Bean has one, called *Store Operations*. Small
businesses usually need one or two, not twenty.

**Ritual** — recurring work that must happen on a schedule and must leave proof: the
opening checklist, the closing count, the Monday deep clean. A ritual is defined once and
generates a fresh run for whoever is on shift. This is the feature most small businesses
come to TechOffice for; it is covered in
[Run your daily checklists](02-run-your-daily-checklists.md).
