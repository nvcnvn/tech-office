Two adjustments the shorter list forced, both in the Maestro flow rather than the app:

- The five-row check walks the list with `scrollUntilVisible` in order instead of asserting

  iPhone, and "below the fold" must not read as "missing".
- The three `scrollUntilVisible` steps that precede a tap on `setting-mute-calendar` now
  set `centerElement: true`. With nine rows the scroll always left Calendar mid-screen;
  with five the page is short enough that it settled at the very bottom, where the tap
  landed on the tab bar behind it and the flow navigated to My Work instead of toggling.
